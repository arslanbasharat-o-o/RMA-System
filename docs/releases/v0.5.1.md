## Executive Summary

Release `v0.5.1` resolves the remaining confirmed release blockers identified during forensic review of the Master Recognition Engine Repair:

1. **Rejected Provisional Model Prevention (Blocker 1)**: Corrected `evaluateCleanerResponse` in `src/lib/recognition/lens/decision.ts` and `synthesizer.ts`. When an AI cleaner marks a candidate as `rejected: true` or provides contradictory reasoning, the candidate model is wiped from both `model` and `suggestedModel`. It is marked as `rejectedModel`, with `decisionStatus: "rejected"` and `displayName: "Unidentified Display"`, preventing any downstream catalog or visual fallback from resurrecting the rejected identity.
2. **Google Lens False Consensus Protection (Blocker 2)**: Added listing title signature deduplication and canonical publisher collapsing in `model-evidence.ts` across major parts marketplaces (`ebay`, `amazon`, `aliexpress`, `walmart`, `mobilesentrix`, `injuredgadgets`, `ifixit`, `parts4repair`, `mengtor`, `groupvertical`). Separated device-only listings from display assemblies and require $\ge 2$ independent screen/display sources for model consensus.
3. **Test Seam Protection in Production Queue (Blocker 3)**: Eliminated `mockInventoryMatch` from `ProcessItemOptions` in `src/lib/queue/processor.ts`. Enforced strict environment boundaries so that test-only dependency injection cannot activate outside `NODE_ENV === "test"`, guaranteeing that ordinary production jobs execute real physical inventory matching.
4. **Single-Product Catalog Support (Blocker 6)**: Enhanced `seeded-inventory.ts` to support catalogs containing a single stocked product with 4 distinct semantic outcomes: automatic acceptance on verified physical proof, candidate preservation for review on moderate evidence, verified non-match on physical discrepancy, and unavailable on missing references without misclassifying screens as external.
